Setting up your card

Ooh La Mahj plays American mah jongg with the scoring card you own. From the title screen (or the card sheet's CHANGE CARD button), either enter a club code from whoever shared the game with you, or photograph your own card — one photo per panel, card flat, good light. You can keep playing while it reads; your card appears when it's ready, with a review screen so you can glance it over before saving.

Playing with friends

Tap PLAY WITH FRIENDS, host a table, and share the invite with up to three friends. Empty seats are filled by the house bots. Friends can join even after the game starts — they take over a bot's seat. If someone loses connection, a bot holds their seat until they return.

The table plays the host's card

Just like a real table: whoever hosts sets the card everyone plays that session. Guests don't need to set anything up.

Common questions

The hint disagrees with me. Tap HINT repeatedly to cycle through your three closest hands, with exact suits spelled out. Once you've exposed tiles, hints only suggest hands consistent with your exposures.

It says my hand is dead. No hand on the card fits your exposed tiles anymore — the app is being honest with you. Play defense, darling.

Sound on or off? MENU → SOUND.

A photo panel failed to read. Retake that panel — flat card, brighter light, the whole panel in frame — and read again. The reader flags anything it isn't sure of rather than guessing silently.

Your card, your data

Your imported card lives on your device only. The app has no accounts and collects no personal information — see the privacy policy.
