OOH LA MAHJ

Privacy Policy

Effective August 2026

Ooh La Mahj is a game of American mah jongg. This policy describes everything the app collects and stores — which is very little, because the app has no accounts and asks for no personal information.

What the app stores on your device

Your game settings, your display name (whatever you choose to type — it can be anything), your imported scoring card, and an anonymous random identifier used to run multiplayer tables and prevent abuse. All of it lives on your device. Delete the app and it is gone.

What reaches our server

When you play at a table with friends, the game moves you make and your chosen display name are relayed through our game server to the other players at your table, then discarded when the table closes. When you photograph your scoring card, the photos are sent to our server, read, and not retained. The server also counts anonymous events — an app was opened, a game was played, an error occurred — with no personal information attached.

What we never collect

No names (unless the display name you invent happens to be one), no email addresses, no phone numbers, no contacts, no location, no advertising identifiers, no tracking of any kind across other apps or websites. There are no ads and no data brokers involved. We do not sell data, because there is nothing to sell.

Visit counting

The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first counter, to understand how many people visit and roughly where in the world they are. It uses no cookies, stores nothing on your device, does not identify individuals, and does not follow you across the web.

Card reading

The card-photo reading feature uses an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) to transcribe the scoring card you photograph. Photos are processed for that single purpose and are not used to train AI models. Your transcribed card is stored on your device, for your personal use, and is not published or shared.

Children

Ooh La Mahj is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date above will change with it, and any new collection would be described here plainly before it happens.
